What AcePrep does

Everything a student needs.
Everything a school wants.

One platform that brings AI tutoring, exam practice, and school analytics together — built specifically for the Ghanaian curriculum.

🤖
AI Tutor
Students get instant explanations on any topic — not just answers, but real understanding. Powered by Claude AI, available 24/7.
📝
BECE & WASSCE Practice
Adaptive exam questions across all subjects, structured around the actual syllabus. Students practice like it's the real thing.
📊
School Admin Dashboard
Monitor every student's performance in real time. Generate branded grade reports and track school-wide academic progress.
🏫
School Onboarding
Schools get a unique code. Students enroll in seconds. Independent students can also access the platform without a school.
📱
Android App
A full native Android app in development — so students can prep on their phones, even with limited data.
📄
Branded Grade Reports
Schools receive downloadable, school-branded grade reports for every student — ready to share with parents and administration.
